Hermes - Privacy policy

Hermes is used only by its owner, to store encrypted backups of a personal NAS in the owner's Google Drive.

It accesses only the files it creates in that Drive (Google scope drive.file) and never reads, modifies or deletes any other file.

No personal data is collected, sold or shared with anyone. The backups are encrypted on the NAS before upload; the data stays between the owner's NAS and the owner's Google Drive.
